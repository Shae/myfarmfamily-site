Care for every animal in the family

My Farm Family is an Android app for looking after pets and farm animals, from dogs and cats to chickens, goats, bees and horses. Keep their care, health, harvest and supplies in one place, and share the work with the people who help.

Get it on Google Play

Everyday care

📋 Care logs and Today

Feeding, grooming, cleaning and anything else your animals need, with a Today list that shows what's done and what's still waiting.

💊 Medications and health records

Track meds, doses and reminders, plus vaccines, vet visits, tests and procedures with their next due dates.

📅 Calendar

Care, meds, appointments and events together in one agenda, so nothing sneaks up on you.

🔎 Kind and breed, with Identify photo

Pick the kind of animal, then the breed. Not sure? Snap a photo and Identify suggests what it might be. You choose the answer.

Places and Areas

🏡 Places

Group animals by where they live: home, the farm, a friend's barn. Each Place has its own animals, people and supplies.

🐓 Areas inside Places

Coops, pastures, stalls, tanks, and Areas inside Areas. Move animals in and out, and log care for everyone in an Area at once.

Supplies and shopping

🧺 Supplies on hand

See what you have at each Place, how many days it should last, and when it's running low. Restock when you buy more, log what you use, and count to set things right.

📷 Scan to add

Scan a barcode or read the label with your camera to fill in the details. When you set up feeding or a med, pick it right from your supplies so the amount goes down as you log.

🛒 Shopping list

Add what you need with quick picks from your supplies and things you've bought before, check them off at the store, and restock in a tap.

Harvest and production

🥚 Eggs, milk, honey, meat and fiber

Log what your animals give you, see it add up over time, and print simple reports. Track costs and sales too, if you like.

Works offline

📶 Saves on your phone first

Out in the barn with no signal? Your changes save on your phone right away and sync to your account when you're back online.

Better together

👨‍👩‍👧 Family plan and seats

Share the work across the household. Each person gets their own seat with the access you choose.

🤝 Sitters and vets

Give a sitter or vet timed access to just the animals and records they need. It turns off on its own when the time is up.

🔁 Rehome and Welcome Home

When an animal moves to a new family, send its history along with it. The new family welcomes it home with everything they need to know.

🖼️ Shared galleries

Share photos of your animals with the people who love them too.
