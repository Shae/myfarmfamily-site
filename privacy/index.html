Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

This policy describes how My Farm Family ("we") handles information in the My Farm Family Android app and myfarmfamily.org.

Information we collect

  • Account: name, email and avatar from Google Sign-In.
  • Content you add: animal profiles, photos, care logs, medications, notes, and sharing/family settings.
  • Places and Areas: the Places and Areas you create (names, types, photos, which animals are in them) and any Place address you enter.
  • Health, harvest and money records: health records (such as vaccines and vet visits), harvest and production logs, and costs and sales you choose to enter, including what you paid when you restock a supply.
  • Supplies and shopping list: the supplies you track (name, brand, kind, amounts, package size, reorder link, barcode), their history (restocks, uses, counts and the reasons you give), and your shopping list and past items.
  • Photos you take: photos you add to animals, Areas or supplies. A supply photo is private to you unless you choose to share it with the product catalog (see below). We don't copy product images from other websites.
  • Contacts you pick: if you import contacts, only the ones you select are saved in the app.
  • Purchases: transaction records from Google Play Billing (we do not receive your card details).
  • Technical: basic logs needed to operate and secure the service.

Stored on your phone, synced to our server

The app saves your changes on your phone first so it works without a signal, then syncs them to your account on our server when you're online. This includes Areas, supplies and their history, and your shopping list. Data on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data; data on our server is removed when you delete your account.

Barcode, label and photo features

  • Barcode lookup: when you scan a barcode, our server looks up the barcode number in our product catalog and, if it isn't there, in public product databases (Open Food Facts and UPCitemdb). Only the barcode number is sent to them.
  • Shared product catalog: if you choose to save product details (such as name, brand and package size) or a photo you took to the shared catalog, other app users who scan the same barcode can see them. Nothing you add is shared there unless you choose to.
  • Read the label: if you ask the app to read a product or medicine label, the photo is sent to our AI provider (xAI) to read the text. The photo is not stored; only the details you keep are saved.
  • Identify photo: if you use Identify to suggest an animal's kind and breed, the photo is sent to our AI provider (xAI) for that one request and is not kept. Only the answer you accept is saved.

How we use it

To provide the app, sync your data, enable sharing with family, sitters and vets, process transfers, send service emails, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Service providers

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage.
  • Google Sign-In — authentication.
  • Resend — transactional email (e.g. invites, notifications).
  • xAI — AI used to identify species from photos you choose to submit and to generate care facts. Photos sent for identification are processed ephemerally and are not stored by us for this purpose.
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases.
  • Google Maps Platform — map previews and time zone for a Place address you enter.
  • Open Food Facts and UPCitemdb — public product databases checked with a barcode number (no personal information is sent).

Sharing

Data you share is visible to the people you invite (family members, sitters, vets, transfer recipients). Otherwise we disclose data only to the providers above or when required by law.

Retention & deletion

We keep data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time — see Delete account. Anonymized purchase, ledger and transfer records are retained for legal and financial reasons.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and access is restricted, but no system is perfectly secure.

Children

Account holders must be 13 or older. Children under 13 may use the app only through a parent or guardian's Family access with parental restrictions, under the parent or guardian's supervision and responsibility. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13, and we do not allow them to create their own accounts. Parents and guardians can contact us at support@myfarmfamily.org to review or delete information related to their child. See also Delete account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may access, correct, export or delete your data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Changes

We will update this page and the date above when this policy changes.
