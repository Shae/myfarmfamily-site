Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

This policy describes how My Farm Family ("we") handles information in the My Farm Family Android app and myfarmfamily.org.

Information we collect

  • Account: name, email and avatar from Google Sign-In.
  • Content you add: animal profiles, photos, care logs, medications, notes, and sharing/family settings.
  • Purchases: transaction records from Google Play Billing (we do not receive your card details).
  • Technical: basic logs needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use it

To provide the app, sync your data, enable sharing with family, sitters and vets, process transfers, send service emails, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Service providers

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage.
  • Google Sign-In — authentication.
  • Resend — transactional email (e.g. invites, notifications).
  • xAI — AI used to identify species from photos you choose to submit and to generate care facts. Photos sent for identification are processed ephemerally and are not stored by us for this purpose.
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases.

Sharing

Data you share is visible to the people you invite (family members, sitters, vets, transfer recipients). Otherwise we disclose data only to the providers above or when required by law.

Retention & deletion

We keep data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time — see Delete account. Anonymized purchase, ledger and transfer records are retained for legal and financial reasons.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and access is restricted, but no system is perfectly secure.

Children

My Farm Family is intended for users 13 and older. The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us at support@myfarmfamily.org and we will delete it. See also Delete account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may access, correct, export or delete your data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Changes

We will update this page and the date above when this policy changes.
