Delete your My Farm Family account

This page explains how to delete your account and data for the My Farm Family app (family.myfarm.app).

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open My Farm Family and sign in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: By email

Email support@myfarmfamily.org from the address on your account with the subject "Account deletion request". We may ask you to verify ownership before deleting.

What is deleted or anonymized

  • Your profile, name and avatar
  • Pet and animal data
  • Photos
  • Care logs, medication records and notes

What is retained (anonymized)

For legal, tax and financial reasons we keep the following, with your identity removed:

  • Purchase receipts
  • Token / store ledger entries
  • Pet transfer records

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Requests are processed promptly (typically within 30 days).
